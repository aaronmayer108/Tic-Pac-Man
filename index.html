GAME SETUP

Players

Player 3 is the green △, moving with IJKL. Still three-in-a-row to win.

Game Mode

Reclaimable Squares

First-to-Finish only: dots respawn after a square is claimed so it can be stolen back. (Percent mode is always reclaimable.)

Speed Boost

Eat more dots than your rivals to grow faster & bigger. Turn off so everyone stays the same constant speed.

Player Collision

Players block each other instead of passing through — run into a rival and you both stop. Back off to get around.

Ghosts

4 ghosts emerge from the center cage and chase you when near. Eat a Big Pellet (one per square) to turn them blue and edible. Get caught and you respawn.

Ghost Stall

When a ghost catches you, freeze in place for 2 seconds instead of respawning at your start. Requires Ghosts.
